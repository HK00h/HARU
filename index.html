About HARU

HARU is a BEP-20 token built on BNB Smart Chain.

HARU has a fixed total supply of 100,000,000 tokens and uses 18 decimals.

The project is designed to provide a transparent, blockchain-based digital asset with on-chain transfers and decentralized trading.
